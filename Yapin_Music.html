How it works

Guess the Song

  • You get a band and a number of lives: 8 on Easy, 6 on Medium, 4 on Hard.
  • Type a song title and pick it from the list. If it is one of the band’s hits, it fills a slot.
  • Find three hits to win. Every miss costs a life and unlocks a new hint about a hit you have not found yet: first letters, album and year, last letters.
  • The hint button is free on Easy, costs 1 life on Medium and is not available on Hard.

Guess the Band

  • You get a song title and, except on Hard, its release year. You have 9 guesses on Easy, 7 on Medium and 5 on Hard.
  • Type a band name and pick it from the list. Its country, genre, formation year and first letter are compared with the answer.
  • Every wrong guess unlocks a new hint: the country, the genre and album, the year it formed, another song by the band, its first letter and more.
  • The hint button is free on Easy, costs 1 guess on Medium and is not available on Hard.

Difficulty

  • Easy picks the most famous bands and their biggest hits.
  • Medium adds well-known bands and more of their songs.
  • Hard goes for deeper cuts: lesser-known bands and any of their songs, with fewer tries and less help.
